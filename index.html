How buying works

  1. Find it. Browse trending tracks and new releases, or search by title, artist or genre.
  2. Pick a license (for beats and songs that offer them) and send the exact amount by MoMo.
  3. Paste your SMS. Once the payment is confirmed, your download and license appear in your Library.
  4. Something wrong? Tap Report a problem under your purchase. It goes to the producer and to ZAMI, and you can reopen it if it isn't fixed.
